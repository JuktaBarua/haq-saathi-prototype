Haq Saathi prototype

A clickable prototype for Ayushman card holders who are asked to pay at a hospital. Tap the outlined areas on the phone, including the tabs at the bottom. Start on screen 1 and follow Ravi's path.

Prototype only: nothing is saved or sent, and calling, camera and sharing are not active. Amounts, dates, hospitals and report counts are sample data. Haq Saathi is a student concept and not a government service.
